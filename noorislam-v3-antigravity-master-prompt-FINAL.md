## 17. FINAL DIRECTIVE — WIN STRATEGY

1. Prayer time pages = just tables, no depth → Google shows them as instant answers only

4. No Urdu content → Misses Urdu search queries

**Hamare 5 winning weapons:**
1. **Content Depth per Page** — har page ko 1000+ words ka resource banana
2. **Schema Markup** — FAQPage + HowTo = rich results = more clicks
3. **Bilingual** — English + Urdu on every single page
4. **Unique Clusters** — Fazilat, Wazaif, Dreams, Names detail — hamariweb ke paas hai hi nahi
5. **Speed** — LCP < 1.5s = Google prefers us over hamariweb

Agar jawab haan hai → ship karo.
Agar jawab nahi hai → mazeed content likho.

**Backlinks ki zaroorat nahi jab:**
- Tum ek topic ko COMPLETELY cover karo (Topical Authority)
- Har page 10x better ho competitor se (Content Quality)
- Schema se rich results aaye (Click Through Rate)
- Site fast ho (Core Web Vitals)
- Bilingual content ho (Audience Size)

Google aise sites ko rank karta hai jo BEST ANSWER dete hain — backlinks secondary hain.
